Andante Pass for Erasmus Students: Travel for Free Around Porto

NIF4Erasmus Team
January 23, 2026

What is the ANDANTE Pass?

If you’re going to live or study in Porto, the Andante Pass (including the Andante Sub23 youth pass) is the most practical and affordable way to move around the city and the Porto Metropolitan Area. And the best part? Whether you’re an Erasmus student, exchange student, international student, or young person aged 23 or under, you can get the pass completely free!

The Andante is a travel card that allows you to use multiple public transport services across Porto and nearby cities with just one card. It includes:

  • Porto Metro
  • STCP buses (public buses in Porto)
  • Urban trains by CP (to Braga, Guimarães, Aveiro, Marco de Canaveses)
  • Private operators within the Porto Metropolitan Area

How does the system work?

The system is based on zones: the more zones you travel through, the higher the cost. But for students aged 23 or under, the price is €0.

What types of passes are available?

The Andante pass is loaded onto the Andante Card, and the price varies depending on the number of zones you select. The most common options are:

Andante 3Z

Covers 3 contiguous zones chosen when you buy the card. Ideal if you’re traveling within Porto and nearby municipalities. (Normally ~€30)

Andante Municipal

Covers all zones within a single municipality (e.g., only Porto, or only Vila Nova de Gaia). (Normally ~€30)

Andante Metropolitano

Covers the entire Porto Metropolitan Area. Perfect if you live farther from the center and attend classes downtown. (Normally €40)

Free? Yes, for students aged 23 or under!

Since December 2024, all young people aged 4 to 23 (up to the end of the month in which they turn 24) can travel for free on public transportation within the Porto Metropolitan Area using the Andante pass.

Who is eligible?

Erasmus and exchange students with temporary residence in Porto
Higher education students up to age 24 enrolled in integrated study programs
International students with valid enrollment in a Portuguese institution

⏳ How long are you eligible?

  • Until the end of the month you turn 24 (general rule)
  • Or until age 25, if enrolled in an integrated master’s degree

What do you need to get the Andante Card?

To request your Andante Sub23 Card, you’ll need a few essential documents. As for the costs, the standard Andante card (Blue or Gold) is around €6, while the Sub23 card with a student profile usually requires a one-time fee between €3 and €6 depending on where you apply.

Valid ID

Passport or European Union ID card.

Portuguese NIF

Your Tax Identification Number is mandatory to issue the card.

Passport-Size Photo

A small ID-style photograph for your personalized card.

Application Form

A completed and signed Andante application form.

Proof of Address (sometimes required)

A rental contract or official declaration of address in Portugal.

Proof of Enrollment (sometimes required)

An official document proving you are studying at a Portuguese university.

Where to apply for the card?

You can apply for your Andante card in person at several convenient locations across the city:

  • Andante Stores (such as Trindade or São Bento metro stations)
  • STCP Customer Service Centers
  • CP train stations (Comboios de Portugal)
  • Citizen Shops (Lojas do Cidadão) – e.g., in Aliados, Gaia, or Matosinhos

How does the monthly load work?

Don’t Forget to Reload!

Even though it’s free, the Andante pass must be loaded every month, meaning you need to activate it monthly (it’s not automatic). If you don’t load your card each month, it won’t be active and you won’t be able to travel.

You can load your free monthly pass at:

  • Metro ticket machines
  • Ticket counters
  • Andante stores or Payshop agents
  • The Anda mobile app (in some cases)

Important: Improper use can result in fines

The Andante card is personal and non-transferable. Only you can use it. Transport inspectors check regularly and may ask if you have your card with you, if your pass is loaded and valid, and if the card belongs to the person using it.

Fines and Penalties:

If you’re caught using someone else’s card or an invalid one, you may be fined between €30 and €100.

Explore Northern Portugal for Free

With the free Andante pass, you can go to class, explore the historic center, ride the metro to the beach in Foz, take a train along the Douro River, or visit cities like Braga and Guimarães, all without paying for individual tickets.

It’s the easiest and most affordable way to enjoy your Erasmus experience in northern Portugal!
