How to Obtain a CRUE Certificate in Portugal as an EU Student

If you are moving to Portugal for Erasmus, an exchange semester, an internship, or a full university degree, you may need to obtain a CRUE certificate after your arrival. The CRUE is an important registration document for European Union citizens staying in Portugal for more than 90 days.

In this guide, we explain what the CRUE is, who needs it, which documents are required, and how the process works.

What Is the CRUE?

The CRUE (“Certificado de Registo de Cidadão da União Europeia”) is the residency registration certificate issued to EU/EEA/Swiss citizens living in Portugal. Under Portuguese law, EU citizens who intend to stay in Portugal for longer than 3 months must register their residence and obtain this certificate.

The CRUE is issued by the local city hall (“Câmara Municipal”) of your area of residence.

Who Needs a CRUE?

You generally need a CRUE if:

  • You are an EU/EEA/Swiss citizen;
  • You plan to stay in Portugal for more than 90 days;
  • You have accommodation in Portugal.

This applies to many:

  • Erasmus students;
  • Exchange students;
  • EU interns;
  • Full-degree international students from EU countries.

Why Is the CRUE Important?

The CRUE officially confirms your legal residence in Portugal as an EU citizen. It may be useful or required for:

  • Long-term administrative procedures;
  • University-related documentation;
  • Rental agreements;
  • Access to certain public or private services;
  • Future residency procedures in Portugal.

Even when not immediately requested, obtaining the CRUE helps regularize your stay in Portugal.

Documents Usually Required

Requirements can vary slightly depending on the municipality, but most city halls request the following documents:

1) Valid Passport or EU ID Card

Your identification document must be valid.

2) Proof of Address in Portugal

Examples include:

  • Rental agreement;
  • University residence confirmation;
  • Accommodation declaration.

3) Proof of Enrollment or Economic Means

Students usually present:

  • University enrollment certificate;
  • Erasmus acceptance letter;
  • Student declaration from the university.

In some cases, authorities may also ask for proof that you have sufficient financial means to support yourself during your stay.

4) Declaration Under Oath

You may be asked to sign a declaration confirming that you meet the legal conditions to reside in Portugal.

How to Apply for the CRUE

1. Prepare Your Documents

Before scheduling your appointment, ensure all documents are complete and updated.

2. Contact Your Local City Hall

The CRUE is issued by the municipality where you live. Some city halls require appointments in advance, while others may accept walk-ins.

3. Attend the Appointment

During the appointment, you will:

  • Submit your documents;
  • Complete the registration;
  • Pay the administrative fee.

The process is usually quick and straightforward.

4. Receive Your Certificate

Many municipalities issue the CRUE immediately during the appointment.

Cost and Validity

How Much Does the CRUE Cost?

The official fee is usually around €15–€20, depending on the municipality.

How Long Is the CRUE Valid?

The CRUE can be valid for up to 5 years. For students staying temporarily in Portugal, the validity may correspond to the expected duration of studies.

Final Thoughts

The CRUE is an important registration document for EU citizens planning to stay in Portugal for more than 3 months. While the process is generally simple, having the correct documentation prepared beforehand helps avoid delays and unnecessary stress.

If you are preparing your move to Portugal, understanding the CRUE process early will help make your transition much smoother.
