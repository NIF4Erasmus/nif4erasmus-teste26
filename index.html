Why you need a NIF

The NIF (Número de Identificação Fiscal) is essential for everyday life in Portugal. Without it, you can't access basic services.

University Enrollment

Required by some Portuguese universities for international students

Work or Internship

Mandatory to sign an employment contract or secure a paid internship

Free Transport Pass

Get a free Sub-23 public transport card if you're under 24

Rental and utility contracts

Essential for signing valid rental agreements and protecting yourself

Bank & Phone

Open bank accounts and sign mobile phone contracts

Cultural Benefits

Access to free entry periods and discounts in 37 museums, palaces and monuments in Portugal.

Portuguese tax identification number

The NIF (tax number) is the first document you need when moving to Portugal.

The following documents are required:

  • EU Citizen Card or Passport
  • Proof of address from your home country

Available for: EU/EEA citizens (incl. Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Andorra, Switzerland) and Non-EU citizens holding a valid EU residence permit.

NIF and Tax Representative

Includes one year of tax representation

€105

* One-time fee for our support services

The appointment of a tax representative in Portugal is a requirement for non-EU/EEA citizens to obtain a NIF.

The following documents are required:

  • Passport
  • Proof of address from your home country
  • Portuguese Visa

Available for: Non-EU/EEA Citizens without an EU residence permit.

NIF4Erasmus is a private service provider offering legal and administrative assistance with obtaining a Portuguese NIF. Our lawyers handle the entire process on your behalf, acting as your legal representative before the Portuguese Tax Authority. NIFs are issued exclusively by the Portuguese Tax Authority, not by NIF4Erasmus.
