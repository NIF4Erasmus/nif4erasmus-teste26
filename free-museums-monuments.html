Get More Out of Your Portuguese NIF: Enjoy Free Access to Museums and Monuments

Discover Portugal’s rich history, art and culture – and enjoy free admission to dozens of museums, monuments and palaces across the country!

Did you know that if you are an Erasmus student, exchange student, or international student in Portugal, having a Portuguese Tax Identification Number (NIF) can give you access to one of Portugal’s best cultural benefits — free museum entry in Lisbon, Porto, Coimbra, and across the country?

Under a Portuguese Government initiative, Portuguese citizens and foreign residents in Portugal with a NIF are entitled to 52 days of free admission per year to 37 participating museums, monuments and palaces managed by the Portuguese Ministry of Culture.

Unlike the previous system, which only offered free admission on Sundays and public holidays, you can now choose the 52 days that best suit your schedule, including weekdays, weekends and public holidays.

Whether you are doing your Erasmus in Lisbon, an internship in Porto, an exchange semester in Coimbra, Braga, or Évora, or a full degree at a Portuguese university, this is a great opportunity to explore the country’s iconic monuments and cultural heritage for free.

How Does It Work?

Taking advantage of this benefit is relatively simple. However, there are a few things you should know before visiting:

1. Bring Your Portuguese NIF

You must present your Portuguese Tax Identification Number (NIF) at the ticket office, together with a valid identification document, such as your passport or national ID card.

2. Be Prepared to Prove Your Residence in Portugal

This benefit is intended for people who are resident in Portugal, not simply anyone who has obtained a Portuguese NIF.

Depending on the museum or monument, you may be asked to provide additional documentation to demonstrate that you are currently living in Portugal.

Examples of documents that may help establish your residence or connection to Portugal include:

  • CRUE Certificate: Certificate of Registration for EU/EEA citizens residing in Portugal.
  • Rental Agreement: A tenancy agreement showing your residential address in Portugal.
  • Certificate of Enrolment: A document issued by your Portuguese university or educational institution confirming your studies in Portugal.
  • Other Proof of Residence: An official document confirming your current residential address in Portugal.

We recommend keeping these documents available, either digitally or in printed form, in case they are requested at the ticket office.

3. Enjoy Your 52 Free Admission Days

You can choose any 52 days throughout the calendar year. Once you have presented your identification document and NIF at the ticket office on a particular day, you can visit participating venues under the applicable admission conditions.

Why Is Having a NIF Useful for Erasmus, Exchange, and International Students?

Your Portuguese NIF is more than just a tax identification number. It is an essential document for many aspects of everyday life in Portugal, from opening a bank account and signing a rental agreement to accessing certain public services and visiting 37 museums, monuments, and palaces for free, 52 days per year on any day of the week.

For Erasmus, exchange, and full-degree international students, having a NIF makes settling into Portuguese life much easier while unlocking student discounts, free public transport passes (under 24), and free access to national museums and monuments.
