Do I Need a NIF to Get a Free Public Transport Pass in Portugal?

If you’re coming to Portugal as an Erasmus student, exchange student, or international student, there’s something you should know before you arrive: you may be entitled to travel for free on public transport during your stay!

Portugal offers free public transport passes for young people up to the age of 23, inclusive — whether you are looking for a Lisbon public transport monthly pass (the Navegante card, often referred to as an Erasmus card Lisbon transport pass) or the Andante Sub23 pass in Porto. This is a great advantage for Erasmus, exchange, and international students who want to explore Portugal without spending a fortune on transport.

However, there’s one important detail: you may need a Portuguese NIF (Número de Identificação Fiscal) to register for and activate the free transport pass.

Here’s everything you need to know.

1. Is Public Transport Free for Erasmus, Exchange, and International Students in Portugal?

Yes! Portugal has introduced a free public transport programme for young people, allowing eligible passengers to travel without paying the usual monthly subscription.

The programme covers young people aged 4 to 23, inclusive, regardless of whether they are studying or working.

This means that if you’re an Erasmus, exchange, or international student aged 23 or younger, you may be eligible for a free monthly public transport pass during your stay in Portugal.

The pass can cover different public transport services, depending on the region and the transport network, including:

  • Buses;
  • Metro;
  • Trains;
  • Trams;
  • Other participating public transport services.

The exact coverage depends on the transport pass and the geographical area in which you intend to travel.

2. Do I Need a Portuguese NIF to Get the Free Transport Pass?

In practice, yes, you should obtain a Portuguese NIF if you want to benefit from the free transport pass.

Although the national programme establishes age-based eligibility, transport operators require identification and registration details to activate the free travel profile.

For example, in Lisbon, the Carris and Metro systems explicitly require a Portuguese NIF when registering for the free youth transport pass.

The Andante system in Porto also requests a NIF as part of its registration documentation.

Without a Portuguese NIF, you may still be able to obtain a regular transport card, but you may not be able to activate the free travel benefit. In that case, you would have to pay for your monthly transport pass.

This is why obtaining your NIF before or shortly after arriving in Portugal can be particularly useful.

3. What Is the Free Public Transport Pass in Lisbon?

If you’re studying in Lisbon, you’ll use the Navegante card — the official Lisbon public transport card and Lisbon public transport monthly pass.

The Navegante Metropolitano pass allows you to travel across the Lisbon Metropolitan Area using several transport operators, including:

  • Carris buses and trams;
  • Lisbon Metro;
  • Carris Metropolitana buses;
  • CP suburban trains;
  • Fertagus trains;
  • Transtejo/Soflusa ferries.

Young people aged 4 to 23, inclusive, can benefit from the free monthly pass.

To activate the free youth profile, you’ll generally need:

  • Fill the form for the Navegante card;
  • A valid identification document;
  • A Portuguese NIF;
  • A photograph for your personalised card.

Once your free profile is activated, you can load the free monthly transport pass onto your card.

Official information: Navegante | Carris

4. What About Porto?

If your study destination is Porto, you can also benefit from free public transport through the Andante Sub23 / Andante Passe Jovem card.

The pass allows eligible young people to travel for free across the participating public transport network in the Porto Metropolitan Area.

The network includes:

  • Metro do Porto;
  • STCP buses;
  • Participating private bus operators;
  • CP urban trains within the applicable network.

To register, you’ll generally need:

  • A valid identification document (passport or national ID card);
  • A Portuguese NIF;
  • The required registration or adhesion form;
  • A photograph for your personalised card, where applicable.

Once you have your pass, remember that it must be loaded every month to remain valid, even though the monthly charge is €0.

Official information: Andante – Transportes Metropolitanos do Porto

5. Can International and Erasmus Students Apply?

Yes! International students are not automatically excluded from Portugal’s free public transport programme.

Whether you’re coming to Portugal through Erasmus, another university exchange programme, or as a full-degree international student, you can benefit from the programme as long as you meet the applicable eligibility requirements.

For the standard youth pass, the main requirement is your age: you must be aged 23 or younger (inclusive).

You don’t necessarily need to be a Portuguese citizen or have a permanent residence permit.

However, you will need to provide the identification and registration documents requested by the relevant transport operator, including a Portuguese NIF where required.

6. Why Should You Get Your Portuguese NIF Before Arriving in Portugal?

If you’re planning to study in Portugal (via Erasmus, exchange, or an international degree), the sooner you arrange your NIF, the better!

Many international students only realise they need a Portuguese NIF once they arrive, when they start dealing with accommodation, public transport and other administrative procedures.

However, obtaining your NIF can take several business days, and processing times may vary depending on the Portuguese Tax Authority’s procedures and workload.

Getting it sorted before your arrival means one less thing to worry about when you start your Erasmus experience. You can focus on settling in, exploring your new city and enjoying your time in Portugal instead of dealing with paperwork.

A Portuguese NIF can be useful for more than just accessing free public transport. You may also need it when:

  • Renting an apartment or signing a rental agreement;
  • Opening a Portuguese bank account;
  • Registering for certain services;
  • Setting up utility contracts;
  • Dealing with Portuguese administrative procedures.
