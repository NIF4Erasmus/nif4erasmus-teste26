Navegante Pass for Erasmus Students: Travel for Free in Lisbon

NIF4Erasmus Team September 12, 2025

What is the Navegante Card?

If you are living or studying in Lisbon, the Navegante card is the official Lisbon public transport card and the most practical way to travel across the city and surrounding areas. Whether you are an Erasmus student, exchange student, full-degree international student, or young adult under 24, this Lisbon public transport monthly pass (often searched as the Erasmus card Lisbon for transport) is available free of charge.

The Navegante card functions as a Lisbon public transport monthly pass that provides unlimited travel across multiple transport services in the Lisbon Metropolitan Area (AML). The services covered include:

Covered Transport Services:

Lisbon Metro
Carris buses and trams
CP suburban trains
Ferries (Transtejo/Soflusa)

Types of Navegante Passes

Metropolitano (€40)

Covers all public transport across the entire Lisbon Metropolitan Area. Ideal if you live outside central Lisbon (e.g., Odivelas, Loures, Almada, Oeiras, Amadora, Cascais, or Barreiro) or plan to travel frequently beyond the city to beach and coastal destinations such as Costa da Caparica, Setúbal, Sesimbra, Sintra, Ericeira, and other coastal spots.

Municipal (€30)

Covers transport strictly within a single municipality, such as the Lisbon city center.

“The Navegante pass isn’t just about saving money; it’s about giving you the freedom to explore every corner of Lisbon without boundaries.”

Free Access for Everyone Under 24

As of recent regulations, everyone aged 4 to 23 (inclusive) is eligible for a free Navegante card — regardless of whether they are students or not (and up to 24 for students in specific integrated programs). This includes:

  • All young people up to 23 years old (inclusive), even if not studying
  • Erasmus and exchange students with temporary residence in Lisbon
  • International students enrolled in a Portuguese university
  • Students up to 24 years old enrolled in integrated higher education programs

How Long Can You Use It?

  • Until the end of the month you turn 24 (standard rule);
  • Or until age 25 if you’re in a higher education integrated program.

Important: Activate Your Youth Free Pass

Young people up to the age of 23 no longer need to top up their Navegante pass every month. However, to benefit from this, you must activate the Youth Free Pass! How? The activation/top-up can be easily done through the Navegante App, at any Navegante Space, or at the Customer Service Desks of the transport operators.

Application Requirements

To apply for your physical Navegante card, you must provide the following documentation:

Required Documents:

Valid Passport or EU national ID
Portuguese NIF
Passport-size photo (small ID-style photograph)
Proof of address in Portugal (sometimes required)
Proof of university enrollment (sometimes required)

Administrative Fees

Please note that while the monthly pass is free, issuing the physical card incurs a small administrative fee, ranging from €3.50 to €12.00, depending on the processing speed requested.

Where to Apply

In Person

Navegante service points, Carris ticket offices, CP train stations, and Citizen Shops. In addition, the Marquês de Pombal and Campo Grande metro stations, as well as the Mercado 31 de Janeiro, have a dedicated Navegante desk where you can get your card issued on the spot.

Online

Via the official Navegante website or the Navegante mobile application.

Card Usage and Regulations

The Navegante card is strictly personal and non-transferable. Transport inspectors frequently verify that passengers carry a valid, loaded card that matches their identification. Traveling without a valid pass or using another person’s card may result in fines ranging from €30 to €60.

Explore Lisbon Without Boundaries

This is an amazing way to save money during your Erasmus, exchange, or international study experience in Portugal. The free Navegante card gives you the freedom to explore Lisbon and the surrounding areas without worrying about transport costs.

From going to classes to visiting traditional neighborhoods, beaches, museums, and viewpoints – all without spending a cent on tickets!
