Shortcut: National ID card as Proof of Address
If your National ID card includes your address written in the Roman alphabet (e.g., cards from Italy, France, Germany, Spain, Croatia, Romania, Slovenia, Slovakia, Czech Republic, Malta, or Argentina), you may simply upload a scan of both sides (front and back) of the document instead of providing a separate proof.

Accepted Proof of Address

  • Utility bille.g., electricity, water, gas, telecommunications
  • Bank statemente.g., Revolut, Trade Republic, ING Bank, regulated banks
  • An official residence certificateissued by a local authority
  • Driving licenseonly if it indicates the address

Important rules:

  • Must be issued in the name of the person requesting the NIF
  • Must be in the Roman/Latin alphabet (documents in English are strongly preferred)

Portuguese Address Registration

If you want your NIF to be registered with a Portuguese address and be recognized as a tax resident in Portugal, you must provide:

  • EU Citizens:CRUE (Certificate of Registration of an EU Citizen)
  • Non-EU Citizens:Valid Residence Permit (AIMA/SEF) or
    Proof of appointment with AIMA or
    Official document confirming your application is pending
  • + Additional proof of Portuguese addresse.g., Rental contract, utility bill, etc.
