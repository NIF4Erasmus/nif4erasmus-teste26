About NIF4Erasmus

We are a team of young lawyers dedicated to helping international students easily obtain their NIF, so you can focus on enjoying your experience in Portugal.

Why choose NIF4Erasmus?

We built this service because we saw too many international students struggle with Portuguese bureaucracy. Our mission is to make your transition smooth.

Lightning Fast

Get your NIF in an estimated 2–5 business days. Processing times depend on the Portuguese Tax Authority and may vary.

100% Secure & Legal

Our lawyers work directly with the Portuguese Tax Authority. Your data is protected and our process is fully compliant.

Student-First Support

We are the only legal service in Portugal fully dedicated to helping international students.

Our Mission

We understand how challenging it can be to navigate administrative processes in a new country, that's why we’re here to make this step simple and hassle-free.

We speed up the process of obtaining your NIF, so you can focus on what really matters: studying and enjoying the amazing experience of living in Portugal, without the stress of bureaucracy.

Our work goes beyond simply providing an NIF. We want everyone to feel supported from the moment they arrive until the last day, ensuring a successful and smooth adaptation to life in Portugal.
